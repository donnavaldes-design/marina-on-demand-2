MARINA ON DEMAND · MEMBER BETA

Marketing guidance that moves your business forward.

Marina On Demand (MOD) is Marina Simone’s AI marketing assistant for entrepreneurs and network marketers. Members use MOD to develop brand messaging, plan offers, write content and organize work around their business goals.

Save your brand and business details in My Ecosystem so MOD can use them across conversations. Review generated content in chat, keep useful work in My Assets, and export text responses as PDFs.

Optional Google connections

You choose which services to connect and which permissions to grant. A Google connection is optional; MOD membership and sign-in are managed separately.

  • Gmail: find and read messages for requested summaries and replies; prepare drafts or send an email after you approve the proposed action.
  • Google Calendar: view calendars and events, and create an event after approval.
  • Google Drive and Docs: find files and inspect their details, create a Google Doc from prepared content, or save a supported uploaded file to your Drive after approval. The current Drive reader retrieves file details rather than downloading existing file contents.

Read operations support your requested work. Writes require the connection’s action permission and individual approval in MOD. You can disconnect a service in My MOD Settings.

How your information is used

MOD uses your prompts, saved business context and relevant connected-service results to prepare responses. Relevant information may be processed by OpenAI to answer your request. Our privacy policy explains Google data use, service providers and deletion requests.

Google features are currently in testing and available to approved Google test accounts. Access may depend on membership, granted permissions and provider availability.
